Evaluation sample

Scoring One Model Response

A short version of the kind of rubric I work with. Each criterion is pass or fail, and a single critical error fails the whole response.

Prompt: "Summarise our refund policy for a customer in three bullet points, under 60 words."

3 of 4 criteria passed. The accuracy error is critical, so the response fails.

CriterionResultEvidence
Instruction followingPassThree bullet points, 52 words.
AccuracyFailSays refunds are allowed within 30 days. The policy says 14 days.
CompletenessPassCovers who qualifies, the time limit and how to apply.
TonePassPlain and polite, right for a customer.
Verdict Fail

Feedback: The refund window is wrong (30 days instead of 14), and a customer could act on it. Correct that figure and keep everything else; the structure and tone already meet the brief.
